Redesign proposal · calendar-first

Three calendar-first ways to show Vanilla Sky flights

Same spine in all three: the calendar stays the hero, scoped to one direction, and every flight you see is a direct link into that ticket's checkout on Vanilla Sky — no preview step, no second guess. They differ only in where the day's tickets surface and how you switch direction.

What changes everywhere

One action per flight. The split "Find flights" / "Buy on Vanilla Sky" becomes a single Book → that deep-links to the exact flight's checkout.
No dashboard filler. The three stat cards and the "Checkout: Official" tile are gone.
No plumbing in the copy. No backend, no "route pairs", no hostname.

Option 1

Focus

One big calendar, the day's flight drops in beneath it.

Single column, maximum calm. You pick a direction on one line, the month fills the screen, and selecting an available day reveals that day's departure right below as a bookable ticket. Best fit for how small the data actually is — nothing competes with the calendar.

Option 2

Rail

Directions down the side, calendar swaps as you pick one.

The clean evolution of what's there today. A slim list of the routes that are flying sits on the left — this is the literal "calendar per direction." Pick one and the calendar re-fills; the selected day's ticket sits right under it. You always see which directions exist without opening a dropdown.

Option 3

Boarding pass

The calendar lives inside a ticket; days are stubs you tear off.

The one with a point of view. The whole tool is shaped like a boarding pass — route codes up top, a perforated edge, the calendar tucked inside, and the chosen day as a tear-off stub. Leans into the scenic-mountain-airline feel. The clearest answer to "make it original, not vibe-coded" — and the riskiest if you want neutral.

My lean: Option 1, with Option 2's route rail one tap away

For a six-city shuttle with a handful of dates, Focus is the honest amount of interface — and it's the furthest from looking generated. The route rail from Rail is worth keeping as a "Change route" panel rather than permanent furniture. Boarding pass is the move if you'd rather the tool be distinctive over neutral. Tell me which spine and I'll rebuild the real app around it.
